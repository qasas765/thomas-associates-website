COMPANY OVERVIEW

Thomas Associates LLC

Thomas Associates LLC is an Iowa-based consulting firm founded by Darrell Thomas, who brings over 32 years of DOD experience, and over 15 years of Commercial experience. His background includes technical leadership roles across CONUS and OCONUS locations, international commands, and Mandus Group LLC. Expertise spans artillery system validation and sustainment, explosives safety, occupational health and safety, federal acquisition compliance (FAR, ITAR, EAR), logistics and lifecycle management, and stakeholder engagement with local contractors and foreign allies.
